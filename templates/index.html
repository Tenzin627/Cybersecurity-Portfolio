About

Grounded in fundamentals, curious about what's next

I'm a cybersecurity professional focused on governance, risk, and compliance, with hands-on experience securing cloud environments and reviewing software for security weaknesses before they reach production. I like problems that sit at the intersection of policy and engineering — translating a compliance requirement into an actual technical control, not just a checkbox.

My interests span GRC frameworks, cloud security architecture, secure software development, and applying AI to security operations — both as a tool for defenders and as a new category of risk that needs its own controls. I care about building things that are secure by default, not secured as an afterthought.

  • Cybersecurity Threat modeling, incident response, security awareness
  • GRC Risk assessments, control mapping, audit readiness
  • Cloud Security IAM hardening, misconfiguration review, logging & monitoring
  • AI & Security AI-assisted defense, model & data risk
  • Secure Development Input validation, secure auth, dependency hygiene
